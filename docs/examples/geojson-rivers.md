# GeoJSON: rivers

The world's major rivers, loaded as GeoJSON lines with `geoLines()` and drawn with `beginShape()` on a dark background, without a basemap. Bigger rivers are drawn thicker (Natural Earth data). Hover a river to see its name: `isMouseOver(river, 4)` hits within 4 pixels of a line.
