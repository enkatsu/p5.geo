p5.geo examples

  • Basic: points written in the sketch, on a map. No data files.
  • GeoJSON: world: country polygons loaded from the web, drawn without a basemap, with hover.
  • CSV: earthquakes: a live CSV from the USGS, sized and colored by its columns.
  • GeoJSON: rivers: the world's major rivers as lines, drawn without a basemap.
  • Geolocation: your track: your location from the browser, and the track you walk.
  • Animation: trip: a point travels from city to city, and the map follows it.
